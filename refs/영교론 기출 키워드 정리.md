## (2018. A. 1) Cognitive Perspective / Incidental Learning

> Declarative knowledge → Procedural knowledge 키워드 바탕으로 모델 파악  
> information processing 과정에서 쓰일 수 있는 learning type 기입형

**모델:** Information Processing Model (controlled → less controlled)

### Keywords

**1. Automatization / Proceduralization**
Transformation of performance from controlled to less controlled.
- **automatic processes** `(PLLT)` — relatively permanent cognitive effort as opposed to controlled processes.
- **controlled processes** `(PLLT)` — capacity limited and temporary cognitive efforts as opposed to automatic processes.

**2. Knowledge Type**
- **declarative knowledge** `(PLLT)` — consciously known and verbalizable facts, knowledge and information.
  - Ex) When a child is able to talk about the use of past tense but unable to use it → has declarative knowledge but not procedural knowledge.
- **procedural knowledge** `(PLLT)` — implicitly known knowledge that is incidentally available but not consciously verbalizable.

**3. Learning**
- **Incidental learning** `(PLLT)` — Learning without central attention to form. Occurs when individual is not making any conscious effort to acquire information or skill but is merely exposed by chance to some experience. / implicit learning
- **Intentional learning** — explicit learning: acquisition of linguistic competence with conscious awareness of, or focal attention on, the forms of language, usually in the context of instruction.

**4. Extensive reading** `(송은우 keyterm master)`
지문에서 incidental learning이 답이 되는 키워드.
Readers spend as much time as possible on reading for pleasure or general language improvement, mostly in real-life situations. Readers choose for themselves.

> Incidental / Intentional learning은 여기 뿐만 아니라 vocabulary나 Grammar와 관련된 키워드이기도 함.

## (2018. A. 7) Sociocultural Perspective

> 학생이 선생님의 도움을 받아서 자신이 혼자서는 말할 수 없는 form을 말하게 되는 것 → 맞는 키워드 기입

### Keywords

**zone of proximal development (ZPD)**
The metaphorical distance between a learner's existing developmental stage and his or her potential development.

**scaffolded help**
Help provided to novice learner from a more expert teacher.

**scaffolding**
Process of simplifying tasks for learners, of guiding learners in appropriate directions, of marking critical features of lg.

**Mediation** (중재, 타협, 타결) `(PLLT)`
The mediating role of language as a means to regulate and control communicative activity.
Learn concepts by three stages of regulation:

| Stage | 내용 |
|---|---|
| A) Object-regulation | They require assistance from **objects** in order to complete a specific task. Ex) 구슬을 사용해서 수학에서의 수 계산하기 배우기 |
| B) Other-regulation | They require assistance from **other people** in order to complete a specific task. |
| C) Self-regulation | 최종 목표. Learners no longer require additional assistance. (learners become skillful, they need less and less scaffolding and are able to perform the activity without external help) The goal of learning process. |

이러한 process 자체가 **two-way street / collaborative effort**이다.

**uptake** (Type of pushed output) `(PLLT)`
Can be a source of noticing.
Student's utterance that immediately follows a teacher's feedback. / What learners learn or report having learned.

- **Successful uptake** — Learners repair their initially incorrect utterance.
- **Unsuccessful uptake** — Learners fail to repair their original error.

## (2009. 24 / 2009. 17) Interaction Hypothesis

> 지문은 NS와 NNS의 대화, meaning negotiation이 일어남.  
> Interaction Hypothesis는 comprehensible input이 언어습득에 중요하다는 점 인정 / how input could be made comprehensible에 초점  
> (2009.17) 학생의 발화 수정 (output) / 교사의 발화 수정 (input)

### Keywords

**meaning negotiation (negotiation of meaning)**
Process that speakers go through to reach a clear understanding of each other. *(2009.24, 2009.17)*
→ Comprehension check, clarification request, confirmation check

**noticing**
The role of attention in L2 learning. Learners first consciously notice a form before it can be learned.

**reformulation**
(interactional modification) (Native speaker's reformulation) (input modification)

**Negotiation sequence**
trigger → **indicator** → response → reaction

**comprehensible input**
Second language input just beyond the learner's current second lg competence (modified interaction).

> **[오답 주의] 3번 선지:** "The NNS is pushed to produce accurate output through meaning negotiation."
> - Pushed output에 관한 설명. Pushed output은 학생의 잘못된 발화를 전제로 함.
> - 여기서는 NS가 잘못된 발화를 한 게 아님.

## (2009. 21) Negotiation of Meaning — Process 예시

> negotiation of meaning의 process를 물어보는 문제

| Step | Utterance |
|---|---|
| A) **Trigger** | She's a loner. |
| B) **Signal (Indicator)** | Sorry? |
| C) **Response** | She stays away from others. |
| D) **follow-up (Reaction)** | Yeah but I saw her at a party yesterday. |

## (2020. B. 4) Negotiation of Meaning — Process 정의

> process를 제시하고 response에 해당되는 utterance 고르기

| Step | 정의 |
|---|---|
| A) **Trigger** | An utterance that causes communication difficulty. |
| B) **Indicator** | Alerts the speaker to of the trigger that a problem exists. |
| C) **Response** | Component through which the speaker of the trigger attempts to resolve the communication difficulty. |
| D) **Reaction to response** | Can tell the speaker of the trigger whether or not the problem has been resolved. |

**Communication breakdown** — 위의 process가 일어나는 계기

## (2012. 17) Pushed Output 분석

> 학생과 선생님의 대화가 두 종류로 제시 / 둘 다 possessive determiner 사용에 관한 것  
> (1)번 학생: 교사의 피드백에도 발화를 고치지 않음  
> (2)번 학생: 교사의 clarification request 후 발화를 고침

**두 가지 측면 분석** — pushed output의 modification이 초점

| | (1)번 학생 | (2)번 학생 |
|---|---|---|
| 분석 | possessive determiner를 배울 단계가 아님 | 이미 알고 있었지만 실수로 보임 |
| 교사 피드백 유형 | recast (implicit feedback) | clarification request (explicit feedback) |
| communication breakdown 존재 | X (대화 흐름 유지) | O (흐름이 끊겨 학생이 자신의 실수에 주목) |

### Keywords

**positive evidence**
Well-formed sentences to which learners are exposed.

**negative evidence**
Information that is provided to Ss concerning the incorrectness of an utterance.

**pushed output**
Output that reflects what learners can produce when they are pushed to use the target language accurately and concisely. It occurs when Ss experience communicative failure and they are forced to make their output more coherent in an effort to prevent further communication failure or to improve interaction.
→ PUSHED OUTPUT does not necessarily include modification of the initial non-target like utterance because it could be a simple repetition.

**beyond ZPD vs Within ZPD**
교사가 학생의 발화를 고쳐주지 않고 clarification request를 통해서 스스로가 고칠 수 있게 지도이기에 → **within ZPD**

**Output hypothesis**
Students notice the gap in his or her linguistic knowledge → modify his output (pushed output, modified output) → learns something new about the language (foster acquisition)

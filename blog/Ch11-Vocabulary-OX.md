# [영어교육론] Ch.11 Teaching Vocabulary OX 20문항 — 해설 완전 정복

Ch.11 Teaching Vocabulary의 핵심 개념을 OX 문항으로 점검합니다. Word knowledge, explicit vs implicit vocabulary learning, high-frequency vs low-frequency words, keyword method, semantic mapping, multi-word units, vocabulary growth까지 — label만 외우기가 아니라 조건과 적용 맥락 중심으로 정리합니다.

---

## Q1. Word Knowledge — ✅ O

> *"Word Knowledge is best understood as frequency, collocation, register, word family, association, semantic feature, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Word knowledge**</span>는 단어의 뜻 하나만 아는 것이 아니라 frequency, collocation, register, word family, association, semantic feature를 포함합니다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**2019 기입10**</span> — word knowledge, depth, breadth 맥락

---

## Q2. Word Knowledge Trap — ❌ X

> *"Word Knowledge can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Word knowledge**</span>는 인접 개념과 구분되는 condition, purpose, learner behavior가 있습니다. 단순히 label이 비슷하다고 같은 개념으로 처리하면 안 됩니다.

핵심 기준: frequency · collocation · register · word family · association · semantic feature

---

## Q3. Explicit vs Implicit — ✅ O

> *"Explicit vs Implicit is best understood as declarative attention vs incidental processing, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Explicit learning of vocabulary**</span>는 learner의 overt attention을 vocabulary에 집중시키는 방식이고, <span style="color:#3182ce">**implicit learning of vocabulary**</span>는 meaningful text 처리 중 incidental하게 일어납니다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**2020 논술2**</span> — explicit teaching과 incidental learning 비교 맥락
<span style="color:#c53030">**2012 Q29**</span> — vocabulary teaching과 incidental learning 맥락

---

## Q4. Explicit vs Implicit Trap — ❌ X

> *"Explicit vs Implicit can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Explicit과 implicit은 attention의 방향이 다릅니다.

| | Explicit learning | Implicit learning |
|---|---|---|
| attention | vocabulary에 overt attention | meaningful text 처리 |
| 학습 성격 | deliberate | incidental |
| 예시 | dictionary work, mnemonic devices | extensive reading/listening |

---

## Q5. High vs Low Frequency — ✅ O

> *"High vs Low Frequency is best understood as meaning-focused input/output and strategy training, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**High-frequency words**</span>는 meaning-focused input/output, deliberate learning, fluency development로 반복적으로 다루고, <span style="color:#3182ce">**Low-frequency words**</span>는 word learning strategies와 연결됩니다.

---

## Q6. High vs Low Frequency Trap — ❌ X

> *"High vs Low Frequency can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. High-frequency words와 low-frequency words는 수업에서 다루는 방식이 다릅니다.

- High-frequency words: 시간을 들여 반복 exposure와 use를 제공
- Low-frequency words: part of speech, word parts, immediate collocation, wider textual context 등을 활용한 guessing strategies

---

## Q7. Keyword Method — ✅ O

> *"Keyword Method is best understood as mnemonic device와 depth of processing, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Keyword method**</span>는 phonologically familiar word와 visual image를 연결하는 mnemonic device입니다. 단순 반복보다 더 깊은 processing을 요구한다는 점에서 depth of processing과 연결됩니다.

---

## Q8. Keyword Method Trap — ❌ X

> *"Keyword Method can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Keyword method는 다른 vocabulary technique과 구분되는 절차가 있습니다.

핵심: sound link + image link + target meaning recall

<span style="color:#c53030">**⚠️ 함정**: Keyword method를 모든 memorization technique과 같은 것으로 보면 안 됩니다. familiar word와 visual image가 핵심 단서입니다.</span>

---

## Q9. Semantic Mapping — ✅ O

> *"Semantic Mapping is best understood as thematically related words의 visual network, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Semantic mapping**</span>은 thematically related words의 relationship을 visual network로 구성하는 technique입니다.

Learner가 이미 아는 words를 retrieve하고 category별로 organize하면서 vocabulary network를 확장합니다.

---

## Q10. Semantic Mapping Trap — ❌ X

> *"Semantic Mapping can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Semantic mapping은 단순 list 만들기가 아니라 relationship을 visual network로 구성하는 활동입니다.

핵심 기준: retrieve · organize · visualize · expand

---

## Q11. Multi-word Units — ✅ O

> *"Multi-word Units is best understood as collocation과 lexical phrases, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Multi-word units**</span>에는 collocation, idioms, lexical phrases, discourse markers, formulaic language 등이 포함됩니다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**2013 Q28**</span> — collocation과 lexical chunk 교수 맥락
<span style="color:#c53030">**2014 기입15**</span> — lexical approach와 chunks 맥락

---

## Q12. Multi-word Units Trap — ❌ X

> *"Multi-word Units can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Multi-word units는 single word 단위가 아니라 phrase, pattern, chunk 단위의 vocabulary 처리와 관련됩니다.

| | Collocation | Lexical phrases |
|---|---|---|
| 중심 | words가 함께 나타나는 경향 | conventionalized recurring phrases or patterns |
| 수업 단서 | matching, gap-filling | formulaic language, gambits, routines |

---

## Q13. Vocabulary Growth — ✅ O

> *"Vocabulary Growth is best understood as noticing, retrieval, elaboration, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. L2 vocabulary growth activity에는 <span style="color:#3182ce">**noticing**</span>, <span style="color:#3182ce">**retrieval**</span>, <span style="color:#3182ce">**elaboration**</span>이 필요합니다.

- Noticing: useful language item을 awareness로 끌어올림
- Retrieval: receptive/productive retrieval을 통해 다시 꺼냄
- Elaboration: 더 많은 association과 context에 연결

---

## Q14. Vocabulary Growth Trap — ❌ X

> *"Vocabulary Growth can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Vocabulary growth는 단순히 word list를 많이 보는 것이 아니라 noticing, retrieval, elaboration이 작동하는지를 보아야 합니다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**⚠️ 함정**: "많이 봤다"만으로 충분하지 않습니다. 다시 꺼내 쓰고, 다른 context와 연결하는 과정이 중요합니다.</span>

---

## Q15. Word Knowledge 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify Word Knowledge only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. Word knowledge는 label만 외우기보다 examples와 classroom conditions를 함께 확인해야 합니다.

실전에서는 "frequency를 아는가?", "collocation을 아는가?", "register를 구분하는가?", "word family를 활용하는가?"처럼 구체 단서가 답을 결정합니다.

---

## Q16. Explicit vs Implicit 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify Explicit vs Implicit only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. Explicit vs implicit은 learner attention과 task condition을 확인해야 합니다.

- Vocabulary 자체에 overt attention → explicit learning
- Meaningful text 처리 중 incidental exposure → implicit learning

---

## Q17. High vs Low Frequency 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify High vs Low Frequency only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. High-frequency인지 low-frequency인지에 따라 지도 원리가 달라집니다.

High-frequency words는 meaning-focused input/output과 fluency development로 반복 사용하게 하고, low-frequency words는 word learning strategies를 통해 처리하게 합니다.

---

## Q18. Keyword Method 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify Keyword Method only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. Keyword method는 예시 속에서 sound link와 image link가 있는지 확인해야 합니다.

예시가 "비슷하게 들리는 familiar word를 떠올리고, target meaning과 연결되는 image를 만든다"라면 keyword method 단서가 강합니다.

---

## Q19. Semantic Mapping 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify Semantic Mapping only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. Semantic mapping은 thematically related words를 visual network로 연결하는지가 핵심입니다.

단순 vocabulary list, dictionary definition, sentence completion과 구분해야 합니다.

---

## Q20. Multi-word Units 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify Multi-word Units only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. Multi-word units는 collocation, lexical phrases, formulaic language처럼 word가 phrase/pattern 단위로 처리되는지 확인해야 합니다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**정리**</span>: Teaching Vocabulary 문항은 label보다 condition을 먼저 본다. Overt attention, incidental processing, word combination, visual network, sound-image link, noticing/retrieval/elaboration 같은 단서가 답을 갈라준다.

# [영어교육론] Ch.11 Teaching Vocabulary OX 20문항 — 해설 완전 정복

Ch.11 Teaching Vocabulary OX는 단순 암기 확인용이 아니다. 실제 임용 문항처럼 **attention**, **frequency**, **word relationship**, **chunk 단위 처리**, **activity mechanism**을 구분해야 한다. 같은 "vocabulary 지도"처럼 보여도 target words에 직접 attention을 주면 explicit learning이고, text 이해 중 repeated exposure가 쌓이면 incidental learning이다.

아래 해설은 정답 근거와 실전 판단 기준 중심으로 정리한다.

---

## Q1. Word Knowledge — ✅ O

> *"Word Knowledge is best understood as frequency, collocation, register, word family, association, semantic feature, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Word knowledge**</span>는 word meaning 하나를 아는 정도가 아니라 frequency, collocation, register, word family, word association, semantic feature를 포함한다.

예를 들어 learner가 *decision*의 뜻만 아는 것은 얕은 knowledge다. *make a decision*이라는 collocation, *decide / decisive / decision*의 word family, formal writing에서의 register까지 연결하면 vocabulary depth가 커진다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**2019 기입10**</span> — word knowledge, depth, breadth 맥락

**답안 패턴**:
`The activity develops vocabulary depth because learners connect the target word with its collocations, word family, and register.`

---

## Q2. Word Knowledge Trap — ❌ X

> *"Word Knowledge can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Word knowledge는 neighboring concept와 대충 같은 label이 아니라, **무엇을 안다고 볼 것인가**를 세분화하는 기준이다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**⚠️ 함정**</span>: "뜻을 안다"만 쓰면 breadth와 depth가 구분되지 않는다. 기입형·서술형에서는 collocation, register, word family 같은 구성 요소를 끌어와야 한다.

---

## Q3. Explicit vs Implicit — ✅ O

> *"Explicit vs Implicit is best understood as declarative attention vs incidental processing, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Explicit learning of vocabulary**</span>는 learner의 overt attention이 vocabulary에 직접 향한다. 반대로 <span style="color:#3182ce">**implicit learning of vocabulary**</span> 또는 incidental learning은 learner가 text 이해나 communication에 집중하는 동안 vocabulary가 반복 노출되며 일어난다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**2020 논술2**</span> — explicit teaching과 incidental learning 비교 맥락
<span style="color:#c53030">**2012 Q29**</span> — vocabulary teaching과 incidental learning 맥락

**답안 패턴**:
`This is explicit vocabulary learning because the teacher directs learners' overt attention to the target words.`

---

## Q4. Explicit vs Implicit Trap — ❌ X

> *"Explicit vs Implicit can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Explicit과 implicit의 핵심 차이는 terminology가 아니라 <span style="color:#3182ce">**attention의 방향**</span>이다.

| | Explicit learning | Implicit / incidental learning |
|---|---|---|
| learner attention | vocabulary 자체 | text meaning, communication |
| teacher action | target word 제시, dictionary work, mnemonic device | input 양과 repeated exposure 제공 |
| 대표 활동 | semantic mapping, word family analysis | extensive reading/listening, graded readers |

<span style="color:#c53030">**⚠️ 함정**</span>: Reading passage가 있다고 해서 무조건 incidental learning은 아니다. Teacher가 target words를 표시하고 analysis를 요구하면 explicit learning 요소가 들어간다.

---

## Q5. High vs Low Frequency — ✅ O

> *"High vs Low Frequency is best understood as meaning-focused input/output and strategy training, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**High-frequency words**</span>는 deliberate learning, meaning-focused input/output, fluency development로 반복 처리할 가치가 있다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Low-frequency words**</span>는 part of speech, word parts, immediate collocation, wider textual context를 활용하는 word learning strategies로 처리한다.

---

## Q6. High vs Low Frequency Trap — ❌ X

> *"High vs Low Frequency can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Frequency는 teaching priority를 결정한다. High-frequency words는 시간을 들여 recycle하고, low-frequency words는 guessing strategies와 vocabulary analysis로 처리한다.

**답안 패턴**:
`Because the target words are low-frequency words, the teacher should train learners to infer meaning from word parts and context rather than teach every word directly.`

---

## Q7. Keyword Method — ✅ O

> *"Keyword Method is best understood as mnemonic device와 depth of processing, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Keyword method**</span>는 target word의 form과 meaning을 memory 안에서 연결하는 <span style="color:#3182ce">**mnemonic device**</span>이다. Sound가 비슷한 familiar word를 찾고, target meaning과 연결되는 visual image를 만든다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**2019_A 기입10**</span> — keyword method와 mnemonic 맥락

핵심 절차:

1. Similar-sounding familiar word를 찾는다.
2. Target meaning과 연결되는 visual image를 만든다.
3. 그 image를 통해 target word meaning을 recall한다.

---

## Q8. Keyword Method Trap — ❌ X

> *"Keyword Method can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Keyword method는 단순 repetition이나 word list memorization과 다르다. Sound link, visual image, form-meaning link가 핵심이다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**⚠️ 함정**</span>: "memory를 돕는다"만으로는 부족하다. 답안에는 **sound link와 image link**를 둘 다 써야 keyword method로 정확히 잡힌다.

---

## Q9. Semantic Mapping — ✅ O

> *"Semantic Mapping is best understood as thematically related words의 visual network, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Semantic mapping**</span>은 thematically related words의 relationship을 visual network로 구성하는 technique이다. Learner가 이미 아는 words를 retrieve하고, category별로 organize하며, 새 words를 기존 network에 연결한다.

예를 들어 *food*를 중심 topic으로 두고 *vegetable, fruit, grain, dairy*를 category로 나누면 단순 list가 아니라 relationship이 보이는 network가 된다.

**답안 패턴**:
`Semantic mapping helps learners retrieve related words and organize them into an associative network.`

---

## Q10. Semantic Mapping Trap — ❌ X

> *"Semantic Mapping can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Semantic mapping은 단순 word list나 dictionary definition 찾기가 아니다. Related words를 retrieve하고, category로 organize하고, relationship을 visual network로 보여 주어야 한다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**⚠️ 함정**</span>: "관련 words를 쓴다"만으로는 부족하다. Visual network와 category organization이 보여야 semantic mapping이다.

---

## Q11. Multi-word Units — ✅ O

> *"Multi-word Units is best understood as collocation과 lexical phrases, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. <span style="color:#3182ce">**Multi-word units**</span>에는 collocation, idioms, lexical phrases, discourse markers, formulaic language 등이 포함된다. 여기서 핵심은 vocabulary가 single word 단위만이 아니라 phrase/pattern/chunk 단위로 처리된다는 점이다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**2013 Q28**</span> — collocation과 lexical chunk 교수 맥락
<span style="color:#c53030">**2014 기입15**</span> — lexical approach와 chunks 맥락

<span style="color:#3182ce">**Collocation**</span>은 words가 함께 나타나는 경향이고, <span style="color:#3182ce">**lexical phrases**</span>는 frequent use로 conventionalized된 recurring phrases or patterns이다.

---

## Q12. Multi-word Units Trap — ❌ X

> *"Multi-word Units can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Multi-word units는 single words의 뜻 암기가 아니라 chunk 단위 processing과 fluency를 다룬다.

| | Collocation | Lexical phrases |
|---|---|---|
| 중심 | words that typically occur together | conventionalized recurring phrases/patterns |
| 예시 감각 | *make a decision*, *strong tea* | gambits, routines, prefabricated chunks |
| 효과 | natural word use | fluency, pragmatic competence |

<span style="color:#c53030">**⚠️ 함정**</span>: Collocation을 synonym/antonym 같은 paradigmatic relationship으로 착각하면 안 된다. Collocation은 sentence 안에서 함께 배열되는 syntagmatic relationship이다.

---

## Q13. Vocabulary Growth — ✅ O

> *"Vocabulary Growth is best understood as noticing, retrieval, elaboration, so it should be interpreted within its instructional or assessment context."*

맞습니다. L2 vocabulary growth에는 <span style="color:#3182ce">**noticing**</span>, <span style="color:#3182ce">**retrieval**</span>, <span style="color:#3182ce">**elaboration**</span>이 중요하다.

- Noticing: useful language item을 awareness로 끌어올림
- Retrieval: receptive/productive retrieval을 통해 다시 꺼냄
- Elaboration: word를 더 많은 association과 context에 연결

예를 들어 learner가 reading text에서 target collocations를 underline하면 noticing, 다음 activity에서 그 collocations를 다시 사용하면 retrieval, semantic map이나 sentence writing으로 다른 contexts에 연결하면 elaboration이다.

---

## Q14. Vocabulary Growth Trap — ❌ X

> *"Vocabulary Growth can be safely treated as the same as any neighboring concept because the distinction is mostly terminological."*

틀립니다. Vocabulary growth는 word list를 오래 보는 것만으로 설명되지 않는다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**⚠️ 함정**</span>: "students see many words"는 약한 답안이다. 다시 꺼내 쓰는 retrieval, 다른 contexts와 연결하는 elaboration까지 써야 activity mechanism이 살아난다.

**답안 패턴**:
`The activity promotes vocabulary growth because it requires learners to notice useful words, retrieve them later, and elaborate them by connecting them with related contexts.`

---

## Q15. Word Knowledge 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify Word Knowledge only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. Word knowledge는 label보다 evidence를 봐야 한다. Frequency, collocation, register, word family, semantic feature 같은 단서가 있으면 meaning memorization보다 breadth/depth로 답안을 써야 한다.

---

## Q16. Explicit vs Implicit 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify Explicit vs Implicit only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. Explicit vs implicit은 activity condition을 확인해야 한다. Target words를 표시하고 meaning/form/use를 분석하면 explicit이고, text 이해가 main goal인 repeated exposure라면 implicit/incidental이다.

---

## Q17. High vs Low Frequency 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify High vs Low Frequency only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. High-frequency인지 low-frequency인지에 따라 teacher action이 달라진다. High-frequency words는 repeated input/output과 deliberate learning, low-frequency words는 context와 word parts를 활용한 guessing strategies가 어울린다.

**답안 패턴**:
`For high-frequency words, the teacher should spend time helping learners practice and retrieve the words repeatedly. For low-frequency words, the teacher should train strategies for inferring meaning from context and word parts.`

---

## Q18. Keyword Method 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify Keyword Method only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. Keyword method는 예시 속 procedure를 확인해야 한다. Target word와 sound가 비슷한 familiar word, target meaning을 떠올리게 하는 visual image, recall 과정이 함께 있어야 한다.

---

## Q19. Semantic Mapping 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify Semantic Mapping only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. Semantic mapping은 중심 topic, related words retrieval, category organization, map/network 시각화가 있는지 봐야 한다. 단순 sentence completion이나 dictionary search와 구분된다.

---

## Q20. Multi-word Units 적용 — ❌ X

> *"A learner can identify Multi-word Units only by memorizing the label, without checking examples or classroom conditions."*

틀립니다. Multi-word units는 phrase/pattern/chunk 단위로 language를 처리하는지 확인해야 한다. Words가 함께 나타나는 likelihood면 <span style="color:#3182ce">**collocation**</span>, conventionalized phrase나 routine이면 <span style="color:#3182ce">**lexical phrases**</span>다.

<span style="color:#c53030">**정리**</span>: Teaching Vocabulary 문항은 label보다 condition을 먼저 본다. Overt attention, incidental processing, frequency, sound-image link, visual network, collocation, chunk, noticing/retrieval/elaboration 같은 단서가 답을 갈라준다.

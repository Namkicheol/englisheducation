### Q1. <span style="color:#c53030;">**Field-Independent**</span> learners are more holistic in perception and perform better in selective listening tasks, as they easily grasp the whole picture of the listening text. (O/X)

**답**: ❌

**해설**:
> Field-Independent: distinguish parts from a whole (→ Selective listening); prefers bottom-up processing. Field-Dependent: perceive the whole picture; prefer top-down processing.

<span style="color:#3182ce;">**Field-Independent (FI)**</span>는 **analytic**(분석적)이고 **bottom-up** 처리 방식이다. 전체 그림을 파악하는 holistic은 <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Field-Dependent (FD)**</span>의 특징. FI는 selective listening·문법 분석·mechanical drill에 유리하고, FD는 communicative writing·authentic reading text에 유리하다.

**⚠️ 함정**: "FI = holistic, FD = analytic"으로 뒤바뀐 채 출제되는 단골 트랩. **FI = bottom-up = analytic, FD = top-down = holistic** 공식 암기.

### Q2. While <span style="color:#3182ce;">**tolerance of ambiguity**</span> is generally beneficial for L2 learning, being too tolerant of ambiguity may lead to <span style="color:#c53030;">**fossilization**</span> of incorrect forms. (O/X)

**답**: ✅

**해설**:
> a) high tolerance: Advantage = free to entertain creative possibilities · Disadvantage = fossilization of incorrect knowledge. b) low tolerance: Advantage = deal with reality · Disadvantage = dogmatic, not creative.

Ambiguity tolerance는 양날의 검이다. **High AT**는 창의적이지만 <span style="color:#c53030;">**fossilization**</span> 위험이 있고, **Low AT**는 체계적이지만 <span style="color:#c53030;">**dogmatic**</span>해진다. Optimum level이 필요하다.

**⚠️ 함정**: "AT가 높을수록 좋다"는 단순화 함정. 너무 높으면 오류를 오류로 인식하지 못한다.

### Q3. If given <span style="color:#319795;">**preparation time**</span> before a speaking task, <span style="color:#3182ce;">**reflective**</span> learners may gain **confidence**, and <span style="color:#3182ce;">**impulsive**</span> learners may gain **accuracy**. (O/X)

**답**: ✅

**해설**:
> If given preparation time before speaking task, reflective Ls may gain confidence, and impulsive Ls may gain accuracy.

Reflective learners는 느리지만 정확하기 때문에 준비시간이 추가 자신감을 줄 뿐, 정확성은 이미 높다. Impulsive learners는 빠르지만 오류가 많기 때문에 준비시간으로 정확성을 보완할 수 있다. 교수 시사점으로 기출에 자주 등장.

**⚠️ 함정**: "준비시간이 주어지면 reflective에게 accuracy가 높아진다"가 아님. Reflective는 원래 accurate하므로 준비시간은 **confidence** 강화.

### Q4. An <span style="color:#dd6b20;">**Authority-oriented**</span> learner typically has **high tolerance of ambiguity** and prefers independent, social learning without much teacher direction. (O/X)

**답**: ❌

**해설**:
> Authority-oriented learning style: needs Ts' directions and explanations; prefer a structured learning environment; low tolerance of ambiguity.

<span style="color:#dd6b20;">**Authority-oriented**</span>는 교사의 설명·지시를 필요로 하고 구조화된 학습 환경을 선호하며 **low tolerance of ambiguity**를 가진다. 독립적·사회적 학습 선호는 <span style="color:#dd6b20;">**Communicative learning style**</span>의 특징이다.

**⚠️ 함정**: Authority-oriented를 "권위를 행사하는" 적극적 학습자로 혼동하지 말 것. 교사 권위에 **의존하는** 수동적 학습자다.

---

### Q5. <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Style stretching**</span> refers to students sticking to their preferred learning style to maximize their comfort and performance in classroom activities. (O/X)

**답**: ❌

**해설**:
> Style stretching skill: A skill where Ss are able to respond flexibly in different learning context by using a new learning style they are not accustomed to.

<span style="color:#3182ce;">**Style stretching**</span>은 익숙한 스타일에 **고착**하는 것이 아니라, 익숙하지 않은 새로운 스타일을 **시도**하며 유연하게 적응하는 능력이다. 다양한 학습 맥락에 대한 대응력 향상이 목적.

**⚠️ 함정**: "stretching = 기존 스타일 극대화"로 뒤바꾸는 함정. stretch = **새로운 스타일로 뻗어나가기**.

### Q6. A student who records her own video after class and writes a learning log is primarily using <span style="color:#3182ce;">**metacognitive strategies**</span> (self-monitoring, self-evaluation). (O/X)

**답**: ✅ <span style="color:#c53030;">**(2010.15 기출)**</span>

**해설**:
> Metacognitive: high order strategies → planning, monitoring, and evaluating learning.

Video 녹화 = **self-monitoring**, learning log 작성 = **self-evaluation**이므로 둘 다 <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Metacognitive strategies**</span>다. <span style="color:#c53030;">2010.15</span> 기출에서 "지문과 같이 그녀의 video를 녹화해서 보고, learning log를 작성하는 것 자체가 metacognitive, 특히 'self-monitoring', 'self-evaluation' 전략을 사용하는 것"으로 직접 해설.

**⚠️ 함정**: Metacognitive의 3 keywords = **Plan / Monitor / Evaluate**. learning log는 Evaluate, video 녹화는 Monitor. 둘 다 metacognitive임을 복수 답으로 처리.

---

### Q7. <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Cognitive strategies**</span> are high-order strategies that plan and evaluate the entire learning process across all tasks. (O/X)

**답**: ❌

**해설**:
> Cognitive Strategies: Learning strategies that are more limited to specific learning tasks and are deployed to manipulate the learning material.

문제 설명은 <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Metacognitive strategies**</span>의 정의다. <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Cognitive strategies**</span>는 특정 과제에 국한(task-specific)되어 학습 자료를 직접 조작한다(repetition, deduction, inferencing, keyword, translation 등).

**⚠️ 함정**: Cognitive vs Metacognitive 정의 바꿔치기. **meta = higher-order = cross-task** 공식 암기.

---

### Q8. A student saying "I didn't ask her to repeat it because I wasn't sure if it was okay" shows a **lack of social strategies** (specifically, failure to use a <span style="color:#3182ce;">**clarification request**</span>). (O/X)

**답**: ✅ <span style="color:#c53030;">**(2010.15 기출)**</span>

**해설**:
> Socioaffective: socio-mediating activity → cooperation, question for clarification.

파트너의 발화를 이해하지 못했을 때 repeat을 요청하지 않은 것 = <span style="color:#3182ce;">**social strategies**</span>(특히 clarification request) 미사용. <span style="color:#c53030;">2010.15</span> 기출 직답.

| 분류 | 내용 |
|------|------|
| <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Social (Socioaffective)**</span> | cooperation · clarification request |
| Affective | emotion control · self-talk · anxiety regulation |

### Q9. <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Message abandonment**</span> occurs when a learner starts but does not finish an utterance due to a language difficulty during production. (O/X)

**답**: ✅

**해설**:
> Message abandonment: 언어적 어려움 때문에 메시지를 미종결상태로 두기

<span style="color:#3182ce;">**Message abandonment**</span>는 Avoidance strategies의 하위 전략이다. 발화를 시작했으나 언어적 어려움으로 끝내지 못하고 중단하는 것. Avoidance 4종: Topic avoidance, Lexical avoidance, Pronunciation avoidance, **Message abandonment**.

### Q10. A student saying "When I didn't have enough words to describe it, I simply used Korean words" is using the <span style="color:#3182ce;">**code-switching**</span> strategy. (O/X)

**답**: ✅ <span style="color:#c53030;">**(2010.15 기출)**</span>

**해설**:
> Code-switching: Using an L1 word with L1 pronunciation while speaking in L2.

"I simply used Korean words" = L1(한국어) 단어를 L1 발음 그대로 L2 발화에 끼워 쓰기 = <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Code-switching**</span>. <span style="color:#c53030;">2010.15</span> 기출 직답.

**⚠️ 함정**: <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Foreignizing**</span>과 혼동 주의. Foreignizing은 L1 단어에 L2 음운·형태소를 적용(예: "공부행-ing"). **L1 발음 그대로 = code-switching, L2 음운 적용 = foreignizing**.

---

### Q11. A student saying "You are so <span style="color:#3182ce;">유아tic</span>" or "I'm <span style="color:#3182ce;">공부행-ing</span>" is using **code-switching**. (O/X)

**답**: ❌

**해설**:
> Foreignizing: Using a L1 word by adjusting it to L2 phonology or L2 morphology. (e.g. You are so 유아tic, borrow your yeon-pill?) / Code-switching: L1 word with L1 pronunciation.

"유아(幼兒)+tic", "공부하+ing" 처럼 L1 어근에 L2 접사를 붙이거나 L2 음운 규칙을 적용하는 것은 <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Foreignizing**</span>이다. Code-switching은 L2 음운 적용 없이 L1 발음 그대로 사용.

**⚠️ 함정**: 두 전략 모두 L1을 쓰는 것 같아 보이지만 L2 음운·형태 적용 여부가 핵심 차이.

---

### Q12. A student saying "pig meat" for "pork" or "thick tea" for "strong tea" shows the use of <span style="color:#3182ce;">**approximation**</span>. (O/X)

**답**: ❌

**해설**:
> Literal translation: Literally translating a lexical item, compound, or structure. May result from L1 interference. (e.g. 'pig meat' for 'pork', 'thick tea' for 'strong tea')

"pig meat"는 한국어 "돼지+고기"를 직역한 것, "thick tea"는 "진한 차"를 직역한 것이므로 <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Literal translation**</span>이다. L1 interference로 인한 직역이 핵심. <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Approximation**</span>은 의미적으로 유사한 다른 단어를 쓰는 전략(squirrel for chipmunk).

**⚠️ 함정**: Literal translation과 Approximation을 혼동하는 함정. **직역 = literal translation, 대체 단어 = approximation**.

---

### Q13. A student who says "counting machine" for "calculator" or "squirrel" for "chipmunk" is using the strategy of <span style="color:#3182ce;">**approximation**</span>. (O/X)

**답**: ✅

**해설**:
> Approximation: Using an alternative term which shares enough semantic features in common with the target lexical item. (e.g. 'pipe' for 'waterpipe', 'squirrel' for 'chipmunk', 'counting machine' for 'calculator')

<span style="color:#3182ce;">**Approximation**</span>은 목표 어휘와 의미 자질이 충분히 유사한 대체 용어를 쓰는 전략. calculator ≈ counting machine, chipmunk ≈ squirrel처럼 **의미적 근사치**로 표현.

| 전략 | 정의 | 예 |
|------|------|------|
| <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Approximation**</span> | 의미 유사 대체 단어 | squirrel for chipmunk |
| <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Circumlocution**</span> | 특징·과정 묘사 | "the thing that makes hair hot" |

---

### Q14. A student saying "put chickens in oil, very hot oil, kind of bake them" for "frying" is using <span style="color:#3182ce;">**circumlocution**</span>. (O/X)

**답**: ✅

**해설**:
> Circumlocution: describing the characteristics or elements of the target object or action. (e.g. 'the thing that make hair hot' for blow dryer · 'put chickens in oil, bake them' for frying)

<span style="color:#3182ce;">**Circumlocution**</span>은 적절한 어휘 대신 대상의 특징·과정을 묘사하거나 예시를 들어 전달하는 전략("빙빙 돌려 말하기"). Approximation(한 단어로 대체)과 구별.

**⚠️ 함정**: 둘 다 "단어를 모를 때 쓰는 전략"이지만 **한 단어 대체 = approximation, 풀어서 묘사 = circumlocution**.

---

### Q15. A student saying "vegetarianist" for "vegetarian" or "cooker" for "a person who cooks" is using the <span style="color:#3182ce;">**word coinage**</span> strategy. (O/X)

**답**: ✅

**해설**:
> Word coinage: Creating a non-existing L2 word based on a supposed rule. (e.g. 'vegetarianist' for 'vegetarian', 'cooker' for 'a person who cooks')

<span style="color:#3182ce;">**Word coinage**</span>는 L2에 존재하지 않는 단어를 유추된 규칙을 바탕으로 새로 만드는 전략(overgeneralization). vegetarian + ist = vegetarianist, cook + er = cooker처럼 파생 규칙 과잉 적용.

**⚠️ 함정**: Foreignizing(L1 단어에 L2 음운 적용)과 혼동. Word coinage는 **L2 어근·접사를 활용해 없는 단어 생성**, Foreignizing은 **L1 단어에 L2 형태 적용**.

### Q16. A student saying "I want to learn English so I can get promoted and earn more money" shows <span style="color:#3182ce;">**integrative orientation**</span>. (O/X)

**답**: ❌

**해설**:
> Instrumental orientation: Predisposition of Ss to learn the lg for the practical reasons such as increased occupational opportunities.

승진·연봉 같은 실용적 목적 = <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Instrumental orientation**</span>. <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Integrative orientation**</span>은 L2 공동체에 통합되거나 그 문화와 교류하려는 동기(예: "영어권 사람들과 친구가 되고 싶어서").

**⚠️ 함정**: "영어를 잘 하고 싶다" = 일반 motivation. "왜 하고 싶은가" = orientation(integrative vs instrumental).

---

### Q17. <span style="color:#805ad5;">**Brown**</span> distinguishes <span style="color:#3182ce;">**motivation**</span> and <span style="color:#3182ce;">**orientation**</span> — Intrinsic/Extrinsic refer to **motivation**, while Integrative/Instrumental refer to **orientation**. (O/X)

**답**: ✅

**해설**:
> Intrinsic motivation: engage in activities for their own sake (competence, self-determination). Extrinsic motivation: anticipation of a reward from outside. Integrative orientation: desire stemming from positive affect toward community. Instrumental orientation: acquire language as a means.

<span style="color:#805ad5;">Brown</span>의 공식 구분:

| 용어 | 분류 |
|------|------|
| Intrinsic / Extrinsic | <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Motivation**</span> (보상·목표 기반) |
| Integrative / Instrumental | <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Orientation**</span> (문화적 관점 기반) |

**⚠️ 함정**: Integrative/Instrumental을 motivation으로 부르는 함정이 자주 출제. Brown 기준으로는 이것들은 **orientation**이다.

### Q18. <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Adaptive language ego**</span> is an L2 identity with **rigid ego boundary** that protects the learner from making mistakes in the target language. (O/X)

**답**: ❌

**해설**:
> Adaptive language ego: Second language identity with which a language learner is able to flexibly deal with identity crisis involved in L2 learning with permeable ego boundary.

<span style="color:#3182ce;">**Adaptive language ego**</span>는 **permeable**(투과성 있는) ego 경계를 가진 L2 정체성이다. Rigid(경직된)가 아니라 **flexible**하게 L2 정체성 위기를 다룬다. Inhibition은 언어 ego를 보호하려는 **방어기제**인데, Adaptive language ego는 그 방어를 유연하게 처리.

**⚠️ 함정**: "adaptive = 적응적 = rigid 방어" 오개념 주의. **adaptive = permeable = flexible**.

### Q19. A student who says "High level of challenge makes me feel nervous, but this tension pushes me to do well" shows <span style="color:#319795;">**facilitative anxiety**</span>. (O/X)

**답**: ✅

**해설**:
> Facilitative anxiety: Helpful tension especially closely related to competitiveness; it motivates a learner make efforts to overcome the sense of anxiety.

긴장감이 있지만 그것이 수행을 향상시키는 방향으로 작용 = <span style="color:#319795;">**Facilitative anxiety**</span>. 경쟁의식과 연결되어 학습자를 노력하도록 동기 부여한다.

| 분류 | 효과 |
|------|------|
| <span style="color:#319795;">**Facilitative anxiety**</span> | helpful tension · motivates · 수행 향상 |
| <span style="color:#c53030;">**Debilitative anxiety**</span> | worry · avoidance · 수행 저해 |

### Q20. <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Assimilation**</span> in the Acculturation Model refers to learners who **reject TL culture** and cling only to their native culture. (O/X)

**답**: ❌

**해설**:
> Preservation: Ls who reject TL culture and cling to their native culture. Acculturation: Ls who view TL culture favorably, but also maintain native culture and lg. Assimilation: Ls who acculturate into the TL culture at the expense of native culture and lg.

TL 문화를 거부하고 모국 문화를 고수하는 것은 <span style="color:#c53030;">**Preservation**</span>이다. <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Assimilation**</span>은 TL 문화에 완전히 흡수되어 **모국문화와 언어를 상실**하는 단계.

<span style="color:#805ad5;">**Schumann**</span> Acculturation Model 3단계:

| 단계 | 설명 |
|------|------|
| <span style="color:#c53030;">**Preservation**</span> | TL 문화 거부 · 모국문화 고수 |
| <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Acculturation**</span> | 둘 다 수용 · 균형 유지 |
| <span style="color:#3182ce;">**Assimilation**</span> | TL 문화 흡수 · 모국문화 상실 |

**⚠️ 함정**: 3단계의 정의를 순서 없이 섞어두는 단골 함정. Preservation(거부) → Acculturation(균형) → Assimilation(흡수) 순서 암기.
